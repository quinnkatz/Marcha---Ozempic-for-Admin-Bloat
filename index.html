    Judgment

    Process, existing tools, keep it human, or don’t build.

    • The answer can be a change to how work already runs, software you already pay for, work that should stay with people, or leave it alone. AI is one tool that can sit inside that call, not the call itself.
    • Economics decide whether a change is worth it. If they don’t support one, that is the finding.

    Before you book

    How is client or patient data treated?

    Data handling is part of the diagnosis. Sensitive client or patient information does not go into a consumer AI plan that lacks the right agreement. Where a BAA is required, a tool without a signed BAA does not get that information. Vendor terms are checked before anything that would touch that data is recommended. Careful claims only — this is not legal advice.

    Implementation is scoped and priced separately after the assessment. The assessment covers the diagnosis and roadmap only.
