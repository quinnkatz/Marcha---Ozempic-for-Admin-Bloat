FAQ

Before you book.

How is client or patient data treated?

Sensitive client and patient information is part of the work, not an afterthought. Marcha comes from healthcare: HIPAA, BAAs, and vendor terms get checked before anything that would touch that data is recommended. Patient and client data does not go into consumer AI tools that lack the right agreement. Where a BAA is required, a tool without a signed BAA does not get that information. That standard applies in a clinic, and it applies in a studio whose clients care what happens to their information.

What does implementation cost?

It’s scoped after the assessment and priced before you decide, depending on how involved you want Marcha to be.

Do I need to know anything about AI?

No. The assessment is built around how your week actually runs, and every recommendation is explained in plain terms.

Will this replace my front desk?

No. The goal is to take repetitive work off your team so they have more time for the clients in front of them.

Is my business too small?

Probably not. Smaller teams often feel these leaks the most, because the person answering the phone is also the person doing the work. The best fit is a team of about 2 to 20.

A short call. Coffee or a pet on the video is encouraged. We’re just getting to know you and the business.
