Do we need to know AI already?

No. That’s the point. We translate bottlenecks into tools your team can actually adopt.

Will you build custom software?

Not in the assessment. We prescribe ready-made tools first. Custom work is a separate engagement if you want it later.

What does the assessment cost?

Fixed $999. Tools you choose run separately — typically about $60 a month for the stack we recommend. If we can’t find at least 5 hours a week of reclaimable time, the assessment is free.

What if we’re tiny?

If we can’t find 5 hours a week of opportunity, you don’t pay. The guarantee is the filter. The sweet spot is about 2–20 people.

Is this just chat-tool tips?

No. It’s a structured ops conversation plus specific tools matched to your bottlenecks — often including ordinary software the team simply didn’t know existed.

Who shows up on the calls?

You’ll work directly with our practice lead. The work is RN-led. When implementation grows, we bring the right support for the job.

Can these tools touch patient information?

Some, on specific plan tiers, with a signed BAA. Others never. The report says which is which, and it says “needs verification” rather than guessing. We are not lawyers and this is not legal advice.
