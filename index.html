Do we need to know AI already?

No. Most of what we recommend isn’t flashy. It’s reminders, deposits, waitlists, and missed-call text-backs, finally set up.

Will you build custom software?

No. You don’t need an app. You need the features you already pay for, switched on.

What does the assessment cost?

A fixed $999. Tools run separately. We start with what’s already in your plan, so the first fixes often cost nothing extra. Anything new is priced in the report before you decide.

Will this replace my front desk?

No, and we won’t sell you that. The tools catch what the desk can’t get to: the call mid-service, the after-hours DM, the cancel that needs refilling. Your people stay the face of the business.

Which software do you work with?

Whatever runs your booking today: Zenoti, Boulevard, Mindbody, GlossGenius, Weave, NexHealth, and similar. We work inside your setup. We don’t start by making you switch.

Who shows up on the calls?

You work directly with our lead, a registered nurse. If you want help switching things on afterward, our team joins for that part.

Can these tools touch client or health information?

For medspas, dental, and outpatient practices, any tool that touches patient information needs a signed BAA on the right plan. After-hours note scribes always keep a clinician’s sign-off. We never recommend putting client health details into ChatGPT or similar. For most studios, the tools never touch health info at all. The report marks which is which and says “needs verification” instead of guessing. We’re not lawyers, this isn’t legal advice, and nothing here diagnoses.

Is my business too small for this?

Probably not. Smaller teams usually feel it most, because the person answering the phone is often the same person doing the service. We work best with teams of about 2 to 20 people.
